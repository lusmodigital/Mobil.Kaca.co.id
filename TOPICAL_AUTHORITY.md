# Topical Authority — mobil.kaca.co.id

## Role and boundary

Mobil.kaca.co.id is a specialist decision-support and service-education property for automotive glazing replacement, fitment, installation, and ownership in Indonesia. It should help drivers, fleet teams, insurers, and repair professionals make safe decisions without inventing vehicle compatibility, safety-glazing status, repairability, adhesive cure, sensor calibration, tint legality, standards, warranty, or price claims. Existing product, model, and installation routes retain transactional intent.

## Context used

- `video-sitemap.xml` contains 740 unique URLs, overwhelmingly `ganti-kaca-mobil-<model>-<place>.html` templates.
- The repository contains 126 index routes: 44 model installation routes, 47 vehicle-category archives, seven glass-position routes, seven news pages, and compact material/product hubs.
- Semantic routes include `/jenis/laminated`, `/jenis/tempered`, `/tipe/oem`, `/tipe/orisinil`, `/tipe/bakaran`, `/pasang/depan`, `/pasang/belakang`, `/pasang/samping`, `/pasang/quarter`, and door-glass routes.
- The homepage identifies a service selling and installing automotive glass for cracked, broken, or leaking vehicles.

## Ignored template noise

- The 740 sitemap URLs are model-and-location sales variants, not distinct editorial subjects.
- Model category archives, author/feed/pagination pages, WordPress assets, repeated metadata, and repeated service bodies were ignored as authority evidence.
- Vehicle names inform fitment workflows only; they do not justify model-swapped article briefs.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and next decision. Triage links to repair-versus-replace and emergency protection; position guides link to construction and fitment; fitment links to installation and sensor calibration; installation links to acceptance and symptom diagnosis; maintenance links to repair or replacement when damage exceeds care; price pages link back to verified fitment and scope.
